Professional Background

I am a United States Marine Corps veteran, aviation maintenance professional, independent entrepreneur and AI systems integrator based in the Mississippi Delta.

My background includes military avionics, experience with General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, FAA Airframe and Powerplant certification, flight training, and studies in commercial aviation and physics.

Today, I apply the same principles of disciplined troubleshooting, systems integration and operational reliability to artificial intelligence.

My objective is to bridge the technological divide between large organizations with extensive resources and independent businesses operating with limited budgets.
